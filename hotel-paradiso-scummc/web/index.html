Hotel Paradiso, Room 714

A point-and-click adventure for the SCUMM engine. There's a party on the ninth floor, and you are in no state to go. Yet.

Play

Click a verb, then an object, or just click an object. Right-click to look. F5 or Ctrl+F5 opens the menu.

Sacra Pasta

The sequel, made with Adventure Game Studio. You finally made it to the party on the ninth floor, and Room 913 has become a shrine to the noodle.

Play

Click a verb, then a thing. Right-click to look. To wear something, use it on yourself. Ctrl+F5 opens the menu.

Both run in your browser with ScummVM.
