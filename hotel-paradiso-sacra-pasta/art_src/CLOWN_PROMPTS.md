# Clown prompts (high resolution)

Six ready-to-paste prompts for Nano Banana / Google AI Studio: two sheets for each of the three
room-service clowns. Each prompt is complete, so paste it as it is.

**Attach to every prompt:**
- `bellhop_walk_side.jpg` and `khuc_sheet.jpg`, for the detail level, the outlines and the scale;
- `clowns_reference.png`, for the costumes and colours only (it's the first game's tiny clowns);
- once one clown has come out well, that clown's sheet too, so all three look like one troupe.

Make clown one first, then attach its sheets for clowns two and three.

| Sheet | What's on it | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | stand in four directions, a 6-frame sideways walk, 4-frame walks towards and away | coming in through the Crypt's archway and going back out |
| Actions | room-service tray, arms up (standing and walking), shouting, bicycle horn, playing an arcade cabinet (2 frames), sitting in the T. Rex seat, talking heads | the room-service and free-play endings |
